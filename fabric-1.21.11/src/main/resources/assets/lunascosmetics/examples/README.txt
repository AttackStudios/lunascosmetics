LUNA'S COSMETICS - MAKE YOUR OWN
================================

1. Open Blockbench (free, blockbench.net) and make a "Generic Model".
2. Build your cosmetic. The middle of the scene (0, 0, 0) is where it attaches:
     - hats and pets:  the TOP-MIDDLE of your head.
                       Your head is the box from (-4, -8, -4) to (4, 0, 4) below it.
     - back pieces:    the back of your neck. Your back is the flat side at z = 2,
                       going down to y = -12.
   The front of your face points towards NORTH (-z) in Blockbench.
   Tip: make a group called "reference" with a head-sized box in it to build around.
        Anything in a group called "reference" is never shown in the game.
3. Paint it, then File > Save Model. Save it into THIS folder.
4. Name the file to pick where it goes:
     hat_something.bbmodel   -> Hats tab
     back_something.bbmodel  -> Back tab
     pet_something.bbmodel   -> Pets tab
5. It appears in the "My Own" tab straight away. Save again and it updates live!

Animations: make an animation called "idle" set to Loop and it plays forever.

Your friends see it too if they have Luna's Cosmetics - it gets sent to them
automatically.

hat_cherry_bow.bbmodel is an example you can open and change.
